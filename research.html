Research

Research Summary

With a foundation in digital communications and computer network engineering, my work has evolved into building applied solutions at the intersection of AI, Extended Reality (XR), and human cognition. I focus on translating interdisciplinary research into practical systems that enhance workforce readiness and cognitive skill development, including the design and validation of scalable measurement frameworks within immersive learning environments. Across all my work, the driving ambition is the same: turning advances in AI, XR, and cognitive science into production-ready solutions that deliver measurable, real-world impact.
