InsightBot: AI-Powered Research Tool

Problem Statement

Researchers, analysts, and students face information overload when processing multiple online articles. Traditional reading and summarization methods are time-consuming and prone to cognitive fatigue, making it difficult to synthesize key insights across sources.

Solution

InsightBot automates research synthesis by fetching, parsing, and embedding online articles into AI-readable vectors. Users query the aggregated knowledge base and instantly receive concise, citation-supported answers.

Pipeline

  • WebBaseLoader — Extracts and cleans text from provided URLs.
  • RecursiveCharacterTextSplitter — Segments long documents into manageable chunks.
  • OpenAIEmbeddings — Converts text chunks into high-dimensional numerical vectors.
  • FAISS — Stores embeddings locally for efficient semantic retrieval.
  • RetrievalQAWithSourcesChain — Uses OpenAI's LLM to answer queries with contextual citations.

Results

InsightBot extracts insights from up to three online articles within seconds, providing verifiable, concise answers supported by direct source links — demonstrating an end-to-end RAG workflow in a user-friendly research environment.

Immersive Crisis Leader Emergency Response Training (ICLERT)

ICLERT closes the gap left by traditional drills with AI-driven VR that captures the chaos of real incidents. Leaders practice split-second judgment in lifelike scenarios to measurably improve readiness.

Solution

Engineered a LiDAR/GIS digital twin of campus (shelter + EOC), then built a modular curriculum — 10 crisis courses + 2 hazard scenarios (gas leaks, electrical failures) — for Meta Quest 3 and CAVE. ML pipelines in Python achieved 95.18% accuracy in cognitive skill classification.

Results

35+ leaders trained; 10+ modules deployed. In partnership with the Louisiana Department of Health, the program scales statewide to strengthen workforce resilience and compliance.

House Price Prediction App

Problem Statement

Accurately pricing residential properties is challenging. Traditional valuation methods rely on manual appraisals that are time-consuming, subjective, and unable to capture complex non-linear relationships in housing features.

Solution

AI-powered Streamlit app using a trained Deep Learning regression model on the King County Housing Dataset (2014–2015). Estimates house prices based on 18 key features: square footage, bedrooms, bathrooms, year built, renovation status, and geolocation.

MLOps Pipeline

  • Data Preprocessing: Cleaned dataset, removed non-essential fields, normalized features using MinMaxScaler.
  • Modeling: Artificial Neural Network (ANN) with TensorFlow/Keras, optimized for regression.
  • Deployment: Streamlit Cloud with GitHub version control.

Results

Users can interactively test scenarios by adjusting property features, instantly receiving predicted prices. The model delivers robust predictions with a streamlined interface accessible to non-technical users.

Validating a CT Survey Scale with EFA, PCA & SEM

Methodology

Dimensionality reduction trimmed 29 observed survey variables to 5 latent constructs using Exploratory Factor Analysis (EFA) and Principal Component Analysis (PCA). Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) confirmed the factor structure and model fit.

Results

Reliability and validity established for VR-learning contexts with cross-validation across cohorts — providing a research-grade instrument for assessing computational thinking skills.

Application

Provides a rigorous, validated instrument for future CT studies, program evaluation, and cognitive analytics in immersive learning research.

Immersive VR Learning: Cognitive Skill Impact

Methodology

Randomized, controlled exposure to VR learning modules with pre/post testing using validated Computational Thinking instruments. Statistical tests included Shapiro-Wilk normality checks, Mann-Whitney U, Wilcoxon Signed-Rank, and independent samples T-tests.

Results

Measured significant gains in algorithmic reasoning, creative problem-solving, and overall cognitive skill development. Effect sizes and subgroup differences analyzed across demographic variables.

Application

Results guide where VR delivers the strongest cognitive returns, how to structure sessions for maximal gain, and which student populations benefit most from immersive interventions.

HIV/AIDS Global Dashboard: 25-Year Trends (2000–2024)

Problem Statement

Despite 40+ years of research, HIV/AIDS remains a major global health challenge. Decision-makers and public health researchers lacked an accessible, interactive tool to explore 25 years of global HIV/AIDS data across regions, demographics, and treatment progress in a unified view.

Solution

Built a production-ready Streamlit dashboard integrating data from UNAIDS AIDSinfo, WHO Global Health Observatory, CDC HIV Surveillance, and World Bank. The dashboard features 6 interactive pages with animated visualizations, regional filters, and auto-generated insights.

Key Features

  • Overview: Headline metrics — 40.6M PLHIV, 59% reduction in new infections since 2000
  • Global Trends: Time-series analysis of infections, deaths, and ART coverage over 25 years
  • Geographic View: World map with regional comparisons across 150+ countries
  • Treatment Cascade: Tracks progress toward the UNAIDS 95-95-95 targets
  • Demographics: Age, gender, and key population breakdowns
  • Country Rankings: Top 10 comparisons with CSV export

Results

Dashboard reveals that ART coverage grew from <2% to 78%, AIDS-related deaths fell 65% since the 2004 peak, yet Sub-Saharan Africa still bears 65% of the global HIV burden — highlighting persistent equity gaps despite global progress.
