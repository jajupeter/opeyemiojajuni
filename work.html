Federated intrusion detection for connected vehicles

Connected cars share their speed and position; a hacked car sending fake messages could cause a crash. I built an AI attack detector that learns from many cars without collecting their driving data, and gives cars that are often out of range a fairer say.

A research finding, then VR apps piloted with real people — each step measured.

  1. 1Research finding
    ResearchFlagship

    VR vs. traditional instruction: a four-year impact study

    Quasi-experimental study of introductory engineering courses taught with a CAVE (design → 3D visualisation → 3D printing) vs. traditional lectures, Fall 2021 to Spring 2025 (NSF-funded, ASEE 2026). Extends my dissertation research.

    866students analysed (487 VR, 379 lecture)
    3.28 vs 3.01mean final grade (5-point scale), VR vs lecture

    Finding: VR sections earned higher final grades (Mann–Whitney p = .008; Welch’s t p = .010), consistently across semesters. The effect is small (d = 0.18), and groups were not randomly assigned.
