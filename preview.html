How I work

Research it. Build it. Secure it. Then test it with the people who depend on it.

Most teams stop after building. The failures that matter in emergency response, security and training show up in the last two steps.

  1. 01 · ResearchFrame the question and the people affectedStudy design, baselines, success criteria before any code.
  2. 02 · BuildGround the system in real protocolsRetrieval, citations and a human check before anything consequential.
  3. 03 · SecureThreat-model and red-team itPrompt injection, tool misuse, data poisoning — and honest limits.Federated IDS · ResearchSDN-based DDoS mitigation for IoT · Published 2020
  4. 04 · Test with peopleMeasure whether decisions improveControlled studies inside realistic immersive simulations.
